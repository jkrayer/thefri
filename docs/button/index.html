As you can see, Thefri has a lot of buttons. Why? Mostly to illustrate how different styles and states can be composed in a complex system. Most actual use cases won't require this many variations and should use a subset of these.

Why a subset? As of this commit, the button CSS numbers 529 lines. While it certainly hasn't been optimized it is worth asking if any given application needs to dedicate this amount of space (bytes) just to buttons. The answer may be yes, but I suspect your mileage may vary.
